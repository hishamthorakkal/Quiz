Rules

  • The timer starts only when you click Start Mock. There is no pause; closing or reloading the page does not stop or reset the clock.
  • Answer editing: within the active section you can move with Previous / Save & Next or the question palette, change any answer, use Clear Response, and Mark for Review. Your last selection when the section locks is the one scored.
  • Section locking: a section locks when its 50 minutes end, or earlier if you click Submit Section and confirm (practice option — the real exam has no early submission). The next section then starts automatically. Locked sections can never be reopened.
  • No feedback during the exam: correct answers, explanations and marks are shown only after the final section ends.
  • After the last section: section-wise and topic-wise results, a filtered answer review with explanations, a downloadable wrong-answer report, error-notebook tagging and a result notification.
  • Progress is saved in this browser only. Use the same device and browser to resume.
