## How Content Was Compressed to Fit

The revised 60-day plan had 54 content days remaining (Days 7–60).
To fit into 45 days (Days 7–51), **9 additional merges** are made — all chosen from lower-risk pairings where topics are thematically coherent.

| Merge | Topics Combined | Days Saved |
|-------|----------------|-----------|
| A | Genetics + Chromosomal Disorders + Dysmorphology + IEM → 1 day | 1 |
| B | Arrhythmias + Cardiology Integrated Revision → 1 day | 1 |
| C | Neurocutaneous/Leukodystrophies + Headache/ICP/Hydrocephalus/Brain Tumors → 1 day | 1 |
| D | CKD/Dialysis/HTN + UTI/CAKUT/VUR + Nephrology Revision → 1 day | 1 |
| E | Malabsorption/Celiac/CMPA/Functional GI + IBD → 1 day | 1 |
| F | All Anemia types (IDA + Megaloblastic + Hemolytic + HS + G6PD + Thalassemia + SCD) → 1 day | 1 |
| G | Endocrinology Rapid Revision integrated into Day 43 | 1 |
| H | Immuno/Rheumatology/ID Rapid Revision integrated into Day 47 | 1 |
| I | Respiratory Rapid Revision integrated into Day 49 | 1 |
| **Total** | | **9 days** |

54 − 9 = **45 content days** ✓

## Full-Length Mock & Error-Repair Block (Days 67–73) — 7 Days

Each full mock: **150 entirely new, original questions** simulating a serious NEET-SS Paediatric Group paper — clinical vignettes, next-best-step, ABG/ECG/imaging interpretation, drug doses, guideline-sensitive management, multisystem integrated questions.

Post-mock report: total score, %, attempted/unattempted, correct/incorrect, guessed-correct/wrong, K/C/R/G/S error distribution, system-wise and difficulty-wise performance, top 10 weak concepts, exact repair plan.

- **Day 67 (Dec 4):** Full Mock 1 — 150 questions + detailed analysis
- **Day 68 (Dec 5):** Mock 1 Error Repair — rebuild weak concepts, revise error notebook + 75 targeted questions
- **Day 69 (Dec 6):** Full Mock 2 — 150 entirely new questions + option-wise explanations + performance analysis
- **Day 70 (Dec 7):** Mock 2 Error Repair — 75–100 targeted questions on persistent weak areas + high-yield numbers/algorithms
- **Day 71 (Dec 8):** Full Mock 3 — 150 new questions, more integrated, data-heavy, rank-differentiating
- **Day 72 (Dec 9):** Mock 3 Error Repair + Ultra-Rapid Grand Revision — error notebook, volatile facts, drug doses, cutoffs, immunization schedule, ECG/ABG/ventilator parameters + 75 targeted MCQs
- **Day 73 (Dec 10):** Final Grand Mock — 150 new exam-simulation questions + concise final error review + last-day high-yield revision
